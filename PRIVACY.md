Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-10-10

This browser extension, "Magento 2 images replace tool" (the "Extension"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we (the Extension) access, store, or transmit.

Summary
- We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.
- Data stays on your device unless explicitly stated below.

What the Extension does
- Rewrites image URLs on pages you visit to reload images from an alternative domain you configure.

Data we access
- Web page content only to perform the image URL replacement on the pages you open. Processing happens locally in your browser.

Data we store
- Extension settings (such as alternative domain, enable/disable flags) are stored using the browser's storage (chrome.storage) on your device. This storage is used solely for the Extension’s functionality.

Data we transmit
- The Extension does not send any data to external servers. No analytics or tracking is implemented.

Permissions rationale
- activeTab: Needed to run the replacement script on the page you activate.
- storage: Needed to save your Extension settings.

Third‑party sharing
- We do not share any information with third parties.

Data retention
- Settings remain until you change them, reset the Extension, or uninstall it.

User control
- You can disable or remove the Extension at any time from your browser’s extensions page.
- You can clear the Extension’s stored settings via your browser’s extension data controls or by resetting within the Extension UI (if available).

Children’s privacy
- The Extension is intended for general audiences and does not target children.

Changes to this policy
- If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and include the new policy in the repository.

Contact
- For questions or concerns, please open an issue in this repository.
